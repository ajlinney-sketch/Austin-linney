07 · Standards

Operating ground rules for the business.

These are operating rules, not slogans.

1

A promise is work

If we say we will call, send, check, schedule or update something, capture it and close it.

2

One customer communication owner

Payton owns the normal communication flow. Dakota provides technical truth. Backup is explicit when Payton is out.

3

Visible communication

For now, customer and third-party email visibility runs through info@homefieldonsite.com while the permanent workflow is systematized.

4

ServiceTitan is the operating record

Where applicable, customer/job status, next actions and commitments live there — not only in email or Slack.

5

Escalate before the miss

If a promise, install date, permit step or customer commitment is at risk, raise it before the deadline.

6

No guessing

Technical questions go to qualified technical ownership. Pricing, refunds and material exceptions go to the correct owner.

Phone calls: leads are expensive and responsiveness matters.
